The video library

Short videos we make ourselves, each one taking a single AI idea and explaining it with everyday examples. This is the part of Ctrl+Care meant to stand in for a course you'd otherwise have to pay for.

Under ten minutes

One idea, start to finish, in the time it takes to make a cup of tea. Nothing is split across a twelve-part series.

Everyday examples

We explain a recommendation engine with a music playlist, not a matrix. If we can't explain it with something you've touched, we rewrite it.

Paired with a guide

Every video has a written version, so you can watch it, read it, or do both if the first pass didn't stick.
