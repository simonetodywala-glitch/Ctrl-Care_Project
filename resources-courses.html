Genuinely free courses

AI education has become expensive, which is most of why we exist. These are the best courses, lecture series and channels on the internet that charge nothing — each one checked so you don't hit a paywall three lessons in.

Fully free

No cost at all. No card, no trial, no locked lessons.

Free to audit

All the teaching is free; only the optional certificate costs money.

Account needed

Still free, but you'll need to make a login first.

We're not affiliated with any of these and we earn nothing from linking them. Prices and access do change — if you find one that's gone behind a paywall, tell us and we'll pull it.

Start from zero

If you've never studied this before

No maths, no coding. These assume you're starting from nothing, and they're right to.

Fully free ~30 hours
UH University of Helsinki

Elements of AI

The best free starting point that exists. Built for non-technical adults, translated into dozens of languages, and completely free including the certificate. Work through it at your own pace.

Go to course
Account needed ~1 hour
G Google Cloud Skills Boost

Introduction to Generative AI

A short, official introduction to what generative AI is and how it differs from other machine learning. Good if you specifically want to understand the tools everyone's suddenly using.

Go to course
Fully free 15 minutes
G Google Experiments

Teachable Machine

Not a course — a toy that teaches more than most courses. Train a model in your browser using your webcam in about ten minutes, and machine learning stops being abstract.

Try it now
Watch instead

YouTube series worth your time

Free, no sign-up, and in some cases better than anything you could pay for.

Fully free 4 episodes
3B 3Blue1Brown

Neural Networks

The clearest visual explanation of how neural networks actually work that anyone has made. It does use maths, but it shows you the maths rather than assuming it.

Watch series
Fully free 20 episodes
CC CrashCourse

Crash Course AI

A full beginner series covering how AI works, where it's used and what it gets wrong. Fast-paced, well made, and aimed squarely at people new to the subject.

Watch series
Fully free Lecture series
MIT MIT 6.S191

Introduction to Deep Learning

MIT's actual deep learning course, lectures published free every year with slides and labs. Technical — but this is a real university course you are not paying for.

Going deeper

These expect a bit of comfort with computers, and some involve code. All still free.

Account needed ~3 hours

Intro to Machine Learning

Short, hands-on and entirely in your browser. You build a working model in the first hour, which is the fastest way to make the theory stick.

Go to course
Fully free Self-paced
HF Hugging Face

The LLM Course

How large language models are built and used, from the people whose tools most of the industry runs on. Free, open, and genuinely current.

FA fast.ai

Practical Deep Learning for Coders

Famous for teaching top-down: you train a working model in lesson one, then learn why it worked. Needs some Python, but it's the friendliest serious course out there.

Proper coursework

University level, no tuition

Real degree-level courses you can take for nothing. Only the certificate costs money — and you don't need it to learn.

Free to audit ~7 weeks
H Harvard / edX

CS50's Introduction to AI with Python

Harvard's AI course, following the excellent CS50 tradition. Covers search, knowledge, optimisation and machine learning with real projects.

Go to course
Free to audit ~2 months

Machine Learning Specialization

Andrew Ng's course, the one a huge share of the field started with. Choose "audit" on each course in the specialisation to take it at no cost.
