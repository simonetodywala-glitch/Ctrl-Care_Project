Our story

Two students, one stubborn idea

That understanding AI shouldn't depend on having the right degree, the right job, or the money for a course.

How it started

It started as a conversation between friends

Ctrl+Care began with two high school sophomores, Simone Todywala and Jhanvi Rapolou, noticing the same thing from different angles.

Simone kept thinking about how much AI was going to shape everyone's future, and how few people around her felt equipped to have an opinion about it. Jhanvi kept running into the assumption that you needed a computer science background before you were allowed to understand any of it.

Neither of those felt right. So they built the thing they wished already existed: an explanation that's warm, patient and free. No jargon left undefined. No assumption that you've seen this before. Just clear teaching that meets people where they actually are.

We're still high schoolers doing this between classes. That means we move slower than a company would, and it also means nobody is paying us to tell you something convenient.

It shouldn't matter whether you have a degree or a professional background in computer science. You still deserve to understand the tools that are shaping your life.

Jhanvi Rapolou, co-founder

More people need to be aware of where AI is going. Not scared of it, not sold on it, just genuinely informed.

Simone Todywala, co-founder
Our mission

Making AI make sense, for everyone.

We believe artificial intelligence shouldn't be the private language of a tech elite. Every person, whatever their age, education or background, deserves to understand the systems now making decisions about their work, their money and their information. Ctrl+Care is our attempt to make that true, one plain-English explanation at a time.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise on

These sit behind every decision about what we publish and how.

Learn

Learning happens on your terms and at your pace. Complicated ideas become clear when somebody takes the time to explain them properly, so we take the time.

Care

We write with warmth and patience. You don't need to already be clever about this to be welcome here. You just need to be curious enough to start.

Include

AI literacy has to reach everyone or it isn't literacy. We build for every age and ability, and we treat accessibility as a requirement rather than a nice-to-have.

Our commitments

What being a non-profit actually means here

Plenty of sites say they're free. Here's what we're specifically promising you.

  • Everything stays free

    No paid tier is coming later. If we ever can't afford to run this, we'll say so rather than quietly put up a paywall.

  • No ads, no selling your data

    We don't run advertising and we don't sell information about who visits. The only thing we ask for is an email address if you write to us.

  • No sponsored recommendations

    When we recommend a course, it's because we think it's good. We earn nothing from any link on this site.

  • We'll tell you what we don't know

    This field moves fast and we're students, not researchers. Where something is uncertain or contested, we say so instead of picking the confident answer.

Want to help us reach more people?

Share the resources, bring them into a classroom, or get in touch about working together. All of it helps.
